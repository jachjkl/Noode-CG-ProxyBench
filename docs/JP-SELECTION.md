# Ordinary and additional Japanese selection

Publication quotas are saved independently for both methods. Ordinary count is 1–1000; additional Japanese count is 0–300. Defaults are proxy 100+10 and direct 300+10. Japanese candidates receive a separate quota, meet the same quality rules and require verified JP geography.

Reserve the best currently eligible Japanese append candidates, retest ordinary candidates against previous ordinary output, then retest remaining Japanese candidates against previous append output. Select the saved counts using fresh ranking. All IPs in one method's output are unique. Ordinary records precede Japanese append records; ranks and text order persist in the cloud.

Automatic publication requires complete quotas and replenishes when competition leaves too few winners. Manual push retests already measured candidates and may publish actual qualified counts up to the saved quotas. Failed competition revokes stale qualification. Changing quotas or quality rules restarts final competition. Selecting zero Japanese append entries disables that additional quota without bypassing ordinary quality checks.
